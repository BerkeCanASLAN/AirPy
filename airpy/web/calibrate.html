Sync test

  1. Switch a speaker on in AirPy (so streaming starts) and set the volume to a level you can hear.
  2. Press Prepare test video. Preparing takes 4 seconds and makes no sound.
  3. Play the video. Every second the screen flashes white once and a beep should be heard at the same moment.
  4. Click the extension icon and change Fine-tune; stop when the light and the beep coincide.
If the light comes before the sound → increase (+) the fine-tune
If the beep comes before the light → decrease (−) the fine-tune

In this video the sound is a short beep and the picture is a white flash. The video loops.
